# Fellowstep

## 0.5.0
- Badges in the quest tracker: green check when the group is on the same step, red -N when you're behind, blue +N when you're ahead. Click one to open that chain.
- Search box for quest names.
- "Ask to Share": ask a party member to share a quest you don't have.
- Full chains are shown by default.
- Fix a finished chain showing as "not started" (and "In sync") to party members.

## 0.4.6
- Fix party members with two-part names (like "Clawdia Owlmighty") showing as "no Fellowstep".

## 0.4.5
- Fix party members not seeing each other: messages could go to the instance channel instead of party chat.

## 0.4.4
- No more "No player named ... is currently playing" spam. Fellowstep no longer whispers anyone; it talks over the party channel only.
- Fixes syncing with players whose names have spaces.

## 0.4.3
- Fix party members with Fellowstep showing as "no Fellowstep".
- Keeps saying hello until everyone in the group answers, and answers again after a /reload.

## 0.4.1
- First public release: see your party's quest chains with everyone's class icon on the step they're on.
